> 🚀 **Zomato Restaurant Analysis Dashboard** :

Analyze Zomato restaurant data to identify trends in cuisine popularity, customer ratings, city performance, and time-based growth.

📝**Description/Purpose** :

This project aims to analyze restaurant data from Zomato to uncover insights on cuisine popularity, customer preferences, city-wise performance, and growth trends over time using interactive Excel dashboards.

🧰**Tech Stack** :

• Microsoft Excel – Data cleaning, transformation, and visualization

• Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts – For summarizing and analyzing large datasets

• Slicers – To create interactive filters for the dashboard
 
• Data Validation & Formatting – To enhance data usability and presentation

📌**Feature and Highlights**

• **Goal** :
To extract meaningful business insights from Zomato’s restaurant data by identifying trends in cuisine popularity, customer ratings, city performance, and time-based growth, and to present these insights through an interactive and visually engaging Excel dashboard. 

• **Walkthrough on Key Visuals** :

**Bar Chart**: Helps identify the most commonly available cuisines like North Indian, Chinese, and Fast Food.

**Line Charts**: Show trends over time for revenue, expenses, and profit, helping to identify seasonal or monthly fluctuations.

**Column Chart**: Highlights niche cuisines like Sunda and Taiwanese that perform exceptionally well in customer satisfaction.

**Bar/Column Chart**: Helps identify cities with the best restaurant experiences, such as Inner City and Quezon City.

**Line or Column Chart (based on Pivot Table)**: Reveals growth patterns, seasonal trends, and market expansion across different countries.

**Drillthrough Pages**: Allow users to click on a category and explore more detailed financial breakdowns for better insights.

🗂️**Data Source** :

The dataset was provided by my institution as part of a project-based learning module. It included restaurant-level data such as location, cuisine type, customer ratings, and time-based records, which were used to perform analysis and build a dynamic dashboard.

Provided By: Excelr Institution

File Format: .xlsx

Data Fields: Restaurant Name, Country, City, Address, Locality, Cuisines, Currency, Price Range, Average Cost for Two, Aggregate Rating, Rating Text, Votes, Restaurant ID, Date.

Use Case: This dashboard can help food delivery platforms, restaurant aggregators, and market analysts identify high-performing cuisines, discover top-rated cities, and monitor restaurant growth trends over time. It supports data-driven decisions for expansion planning, customer targeting, and operational improvements.
